Checking every customer by hand?

If you ship goods abroad, every new customer and every consignee should be checked before the goods leave. US sanctions penalties apply even when you didn't know. Government search sites check one name at a time, and they keep no record.

Hugo Trade Shield is a small app that sits by your mouse on your Windows PC and does the checking with you.

1

Box any name, anywhere

A name in an email, a booking form or an invoice PDF: draw a box around it and Hugo checks it on the spot. No copy and paste.

2

Check your whole customer list

Drop in your customer and consignee list as Excel or CSV. Every name is checked, with no limit and no fee per check.

3

Re-checks when the lists change

The lists change every week. Hugo re-checks everyone you have already cleared and tells you only what changed.

4

Proof for every check

A dated record of each check: which name, which lists, which version of the lists, and who cleared it. Ready if anyone asks.

5

Your customer list stays with you

The lists come to your PC and the matching happens there. Your customer names are never uploaded to us.

6

Set up in two minutes

Download, open, start checking. No integration project, no contract, cancel any month.
